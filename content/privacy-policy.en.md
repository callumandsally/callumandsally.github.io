At *Callum & Sally*, we respect the privacy of our visitors and are committed to handling personal information responsibly.

#### 🔒 **Personal Information**

This website does not provide commenting facilities or user registration.

Visitors are not required to provide personal information, such as their name or email address, in order to browse this website.

However, technical information relating to website access may be processed by external services for purposes such as website delivery, security and analytics.

#### 🌐 **Website Hosting and Content Delivery**

This website uses Cloudflare Pages for website hosting and Cloudflare R2 for storing and delivering audio files.

Cloudflare may process and log information such as IP addresses and traffic data for website and content delivery, security management and related purposes.

The web fonts used on this website are stored and served locally from our own website.

For further information about how Cloudflare handles data, please refer to [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

#### 📊 **Website Analytics**

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the site is used and to improve the browsing experience.

Through this service, we review statistical information such as page views, the countries and regions from which visitors access the site, and page loading times.

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies for analytics or track individuals across websites.

For further details, please refer to the [Cloudflare Web Analytics documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/about/).

#### 🍪 **Cookies**

This website does not use cookies for advertising, analytics or visitor tracking.

However, Cloudflare's security features may use cookies to maintain website security and prevent unauthorised access.

External services linked from this website may also use cookies in accordance with their own policies.

#### 💌 **Contact and Enquiries**

We use Google Forms to receive enquiries.

The contact form allows you to provide the following information:

- Name (optional)
- Email address (optional)
- Message (required)
- Whether you would like a reply (required)

Email addresses are not collected automatically, and you are welcome to contact us anonymously.

The information you provide is used to respond to enquiries, communicate with you where appropriate and maintain necessary records.

We handle this information with care, retain it for as long as necessary for these purposes, and delete personal information when it is no longer needed.

We may use AI services to assist with translating and understanding enquiries or drafting replies. Before doing so, we remove names, email addresses and other information that could identify the individual concerned.

We will obtain your permission before publishing the contents of your message on this website or through any other medium.

Apart from the processing necessary to provide these services and the uses described above, we will not disclose your personal information to third parties without your consent, unless required by law.

For information about how Google handles data in connection with Google Forms, please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en-GB).

You can contact us using [this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSef8ux-1LbxaQydvmMRDspBkr8-vdhBz9B9VfvhRDEotQUFoA/viewform?usp=dialog).

#### 🗂️ **Data Management, Access, Correction and Deletion**

We take reasonable care to protect personal information from unauthorised disclosure, loss and misuse.

If you wish to access, correct or request the deletion of personal information you have provided, please contact us using the contact form. We will respond in accordance with applicable laws after verifying your identity.
