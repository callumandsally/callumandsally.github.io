**The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** are a set of 17 global goals established by the United Nations in 2015.
They are not only pursued by governments and NGOs, but also by private companies around the world.

Each of these goals addresses urgent global challenges, and achieving them requires active contribution—not only from nations and organisations, but from individuals as well.

We strive to make a small but meaningful contribution through our daily lives.
On this site, we aim to support a wide range of goals by including links to click-to-donate initiatives at the end of our blog posts.
